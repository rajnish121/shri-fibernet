Internet 101

Mbps is how much data moves each second

Megabits per second. A higher number moves more data at once, so more people and devices can share the connection without slowing each other down. 1 Gbps is 1000 Mbps.

Fiber sends data as light

Light pulses travel through thin glass strands, so fiber holds steady, high speeds that older copper cable struggles to match. It suits streaming, video calls and online classes.

Small changes make Wi-Fi faster

  • Put the router in the middle of the home, up high and in the open
  • Use 5 GHz close to the router for the best speed
  • Use 2.4 GHz for rooms farther away
  • Use a LAN cable for TVs and desktops
