Questions people ask

Is the internet really unlimited?

Yes. All eight plans, from 50 Mbps to 1 Gbps, are unlimited.

Do I pay for installation?

Installation is zero-cost on all plans.* Message us on WhatsApp to confirm for your address.

Do I have to buy a router?

No. A dual band router is free to use* with your connection.

What do I get besides internet?

350 IPTV channels, 20 OTT apps and a free landline connection.*

Which duration should I choose?

Longer plans cost less each month. Use the duration switch on the Plans page to compare.

How do I get a connection?

Tap any Get started button. It opens WhatsApp with your plan already written. Send it with your address and our team takes it from there.
